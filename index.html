Methodology

The Five Milestones

Every commission follows our structured architectural framework, ensuring uncompromising design integrity from blank site to keys in hand.

01 / DISCOVER

Vision, Site & Lifestyle Alignment

We spend days examining the soil, wind trajectory, light angles, and your familial rhythms. We establish programmatic requirements, municipal parameters, and budget guardrails.
02 / CONCEPT

Volumetric Massing & Spatial Flow

Development of hand-sketched layouts, physical timber scale models, daylight diagrams, and spatial mood palettes to solidify the soul of the home before computer CAD begins.
03 / DESIGN

Micro-Detailing, BIM & Materials

Millimeter-accurate working drawings, MEP coordination, 3D photorealistic lighting studies, and physical sample boards presenting stone slabs, timber finishes, and metals.
04 / BUILD

Turnkey Construction & Oversight

Our full-time site engineers direct structural concrete pouring, masonry, waterproofing, bespoke millwork, and stone installation with weekly cloud progress audits.
05 / DELIVER

Fine Commissioning & Handover

Snagging verification, professional architectural documentation photography, air quality balancing, furniture placement, and complete operations manuals delivered to you.
Philosophy of Substance

Honest Materiality

We select materials that do not deteriorate with time; rather, they age gracefully, acquiring depth, patina, and memory.

01 · Earth

Stone

Kota brown stone, Italian silver travertine, and raw riverbed basalt selected directly from regional quarries.

Thermal Coolness
02 · Grain

Wood

Reclaimed 60-year-old Indian teak, white oak, and charred cedar treated only with biological hardwax oils.

Tactile Warmth
03 · Alloy

Metal

Hand-rubbed patinated bronze, blackened mild steel, and unlacquered brass hardware that reflects human touch.

Living Patina
04 · Light

Glass

Low-iron structural glazing, acoustic fluted panels, and motorized double-glazed sliders to invite external foliage indoors.

Diffused Daylight
05 · Weave

Textiles

Handloom Belgian linens, raw Indian tussar silks, and bouclé wools custom dyed to neutral earthen pigments.
